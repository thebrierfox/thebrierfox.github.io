Common questions

How does it know where I am?

Your phone's GPS. We check if you're within range of a business that's hiring — that's all we do with your location. We don't store your location history.

Do I have to submit a resume?

No. Rollby just shows you the job. How you apply is up to the business — some want you to walk in, some have a phone number, some have an application link.

Does this drain my battery?

Minimal impact. Rollby uses low-power background location checks — the same technology your weather app uses for local forecasts.

I'm a business. How many people will see my listing?

It depends on how many enrolled users pass your address. Rollby is growing its user base in Poplar Bluff now — early listings get the most exposure as the network grows.
